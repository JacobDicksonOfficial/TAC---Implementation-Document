\section{Summary and Closing Remarks}

This report has presented the Semester One phase of the TAC Industries Final Year Project, focusing on the design, research, and architectural validation of a secure infrastructure and case management platform. Through the application of Zero Trust principles, identity-centric access control, and separation of duties, the project demonstrates how security and governance can be embedded directly into system design.

The work completed in Semester One establishes a robust foundation for continued development, balancing academic requirements with realistic enterprise practices. By prioritising architectural correctness, ethical awareness, and controlled scope, the project is well positioned to progress into implementation and refinement during Semester Two.
